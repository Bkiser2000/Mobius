# Contributing to MobiusPL

Thank you for your interest in MobiusPL! This document outlines how to contribute.

## Licensing

By contributing to MobiusPL, you agree that your contributions will be licensed under 
the AGPL v3 license. You also grant the maintainers the right to offer commercial 
licenses for your contributions.

## Development Setup

### Prerequisites
- GCC/Clang with C11 support
- Make
- OpenSSL development libraries (`libssl-dev`)
- Docker (optional, for containerized testing)
